`<loomi-button-group-item>` gains `tooltip` and `tooltip-position` (default `top`) for a built-in `<loomi-tooltip>` on hover and keyboard focus, so items no longer need wrapping in `<loomi-tooltip>`. Selection, sizing and the segmented look are unchanged. Tooltip text that differs from the item's accessible name is also exposed as its description. Adds a dependency on `@loomidev/tooltip`.
